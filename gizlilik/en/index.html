Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026
Data controller: Umay Yentur (individual developer) · Contact: umay.yentur@gmail.com

BLOCU is a crossword game where you find English words from Turkish clues. There are no accounts or sign-ups, and the app has no server of ours. The app does not send your game data to us.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in a database on your device and is not sent to us: your game progress (levels, stars, daily results, Tempo times), streak and streak freezes, achievements, gold balance and transactions, hint tokens, placement result, review and spelling practice records, and your theme and notification preferences.

  • Notifications (optional; you can turn them off in Settings) are scheduled on your device; no data leaves your device for them. On Android the boot permission is used to restore reminders after the device restarts.
  • Photos (iOS only): if you choose to save the share card, the app asks only for add permission; it never reads your photo library.
  • You can delete this data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

2. Sharing

You share your daily result or share card only when you tap "Share", with the app you choose. The shared content does not include answer letters. Sharing does not go through us.

3. Ads and Google

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for consent. Through these SDKs Google may automatically collect and process the following for ad delivery, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address (approximate location may be derived from it),
  • in-app interactions (app launch, ad views and taps),
  • diagnostics (such as launch time and hang rate),
  • device identifiers: on Android the advertising ID and app set ID; on iOS the identifier for vendor (IDFV).

On iOS the app does not ask for tracking permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device settings. Data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses data: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google UMP before requesting ads; you can change your choice in Settings → "Privacy options". If you do not consent, non-personalised or limited ads are shown, or no ads at all.
  • Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

4. Crash reports

The app contains no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. If you allowed it in your device settings, Google Play and Apple may share anonymous crash and performance information they collect at the operating-system level with the developer. You can turn this off in your device settings.

5. Purchases

When in-app purchases (ad removal; gold and hint token packs) are enabled, payment is processed by Google Play or the App Store. Your card or payment details never reach us. The result of a purchase (for example, ads being removed) is stored on your device.

6. Beta report (beta builds only)

In beta builds you may create an anonymous usage summary in Settings and share it yourself. It contains:

  • session and level counts, economy and ad counters;
  • attempt and error counts per word (words by their content identifiers);
  • per-day app opens and screen-flow counters (hint menu, notifications, sharing, spelling practice, login reward, hint tokens, Shop, streak freeze, help, tour, achievements);
  • summaries of placement, review, chain runs and practice sessions;
  • app and content version, operating-system version and screen class.

It contains no device ID, typed letters, exact timestamps, dates or personal information (days are counted from the first day only; each report gets a random identifier of its own). You preview it before sharing and it goes only to the app or person you choose. The app never sends it by itself. This sharing is declared as optional data in the Google Play Data safety form.

7. Information under KVKK and the GDPR

  • Purpose and legal basis: Data kept on your device exists for the game to work and never reaches the developer. Data that Google processes through the SDKs is processed to show and measure ads so the app can be offered for free; the legal basis is legitimate interest, and consent for personalised ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland (KVKK Art. 5; GDPR Art. 6).
  • Transfers: Data collected by Google may be processed on Google's servers outside Türkiye, including the United States (KVKK Art. 9). Google acts as a separate controller for this processing under its own terms.
  • Retention: Data on your device stays there until you delete it. Retention of data held by Google follows Google's policies.
  • Your rights: Under KVKK Art. 11 and the GDPR you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, rectification or erasure, object, and claim compensation for damage. The developer holds no data about you on any server; for data processed by Google you can also use Google's tools.
  • Requests: Write to umay.yentur@gmail.com; you will get an answer within 30 days (KVKK Art. 13). If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or, in the EEA, to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is for users aged 13 and over; it is not directed to children and does not knowingly process data of children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the effective date changes.
